SANITIZATION (public edition, 2026-11-19)

This file lists categories of change. It does not restate what was removed.

data/*.jsonl
  - The lab client recorded two extra fields per row: the model-server port,
    and the live-service health at that moment. Both were dropped. Every
    other field, including the generated text, is copied unchanged, so the
    byte-identity counts reproduce from these files.

tools/client.py, tools/deep.py
  - The model-server host now comes from JUPI_MODEL_HOST (default localhost)
    and the port from the command line. The live-service health probe was
    removed. Sampling and request bodies are unchanged.

README.md command line
  - Home-directory paths, the loopback address and the port are shown as
    placeholders.

Not published
  - Lab receipts, node addresses, access paths, ssh configuration, unit-file
    hashes, and the internal sortie reports these numbers come from.

data/prompts20.json, data/deep_rows.json, data/deep-prompt-2048.json
  - Synthetic test inputs, published unchanged.
